## What we ask of you

1. Check each row of the tables below: the drug, the condition, the threshold, the verdict
   (status and risk tier) and the cited source. Mark each as *confirmed*, *changed* or *remove*.
2. Answer the numbered questions at the end.
3. Say which numeric doses may be shown. Until a dose is signed off, the card shows
   "Renal adjustment required — consult SmPC" or "Dose: consult SmPC" and no number.

How to read the sheet:

- **Status** order: `NO_ISSUE` < `WARNING` < `BLOCKED`. **Tier**: `LOW` < `MODERATE` < `HIGH` <
  `CRITICAL`. The model can only raise a verdict, never lower it.
- **Renal rules** fire when the value is strictly below the threshold (equal does not fire).
  CrCl is Cockcroft-Gault with actual body weight; eGFR is the lab value as entered.
- **Citations** name a product and section of the Spanish SmPC (AEMPS CIMA,
  https://cima.aemps.es) or a published guideline. They were written without access to the live
  documents and contain no deep links: please verify them.
- The tool checks orders against coded diagnoses and the note. It never makes diagnoses and
  never says an order is approved or safe.

2. Drug list: is this the right set of about 15 drugs for the demo (Q-5)? Anything to add or remove, e.g. a second alternative analgesic?
3. Renal thresholds: confirm each threshold, its basis (CrCl by Cockcroft-Gault or eGFR) and its status. In particular dexketoprofen BLOCKED below CrCl 60 (the SmPC says 59 or lower) and metformin BLOCKED below eGFR 30.

5. Cockcroft-Gault uses actual body weight, creatinine in mg/dL, a 0.85 factor for women, and compares the unrounded value with the threshold (the card shows one decimal). Is that acceptable, or should obese or low-weight patients use another weight?
6. Preset B dose guidance: the ciprofloxacin text 'maximum 500 mg a day by mouth' is hidden until you sign it off (Q-5). Confirm or correct it, or keep the fallback 'consult SmPC'.
